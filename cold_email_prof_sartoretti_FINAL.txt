About My Work:
At Ansys, I have developed a modular VLA pipeline for robotic manipulation (https://github.com/maneetchatterjee/maneet) that includes:
- Vision-based perception with structured scene representations
- Natural language command parsing for robot tasks
- Symbolic planning with collision avoidance
- Custom inverse kinematics implementation
- Comprehensive evaluation metrics and testing framework

The system demonstrates strong performance in single-agent pick-and-place scenarios within simulation environments using PyBullet.

Research Interest:
I am deeply interested in multi-agent robotics and coordination. Your research on decentralized multi-agent path finding has inspired me to think about how language-driven task specification could scale to multi-robot systems. Some questions I find compelling:

1. How can multiple robots with heterogeneous capabilities collectively understand and execute complex, language-specified tasks?
2. What communication protocols enable efficient coordination when agents operate on abstract task descriptions rather than explicit waypoints?
3. How can we develop benchmarks that meaningfully evaluate multi-agent manipulation systems?

Why MARMot Lab:
