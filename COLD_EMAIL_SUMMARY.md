## Methodology: Devil's Advocate Approach

The email was developed using a rigorous iterative process:

1. **Initial Draft** - Baseline email
2. **Self-Review** - Identify weaknesses
3. **Professor's Perspective** - Review as if receiving the email
4. **Academic Panel Review** - Evaluate from admissions/selection perspective
5. **Refinement** - Incorporate all feedback
6. **Quality Assessment** - Final evaluation

### Review Criteria Used

**From Professor's Perspective:**
- Does it show genuine understanding of my research?
- Is the student technically competent?
- Are they genuinely interested or sending generic emails?
- What specific value would they add to the lab?
- Is this worth responding to?

**From Academic Panel Perspective:**
- Authenticity (genuine vs. template)
- Quality (writing, structure, professionalism)
- Relevance (fit with lab research)
- Specificity (concrete vs. vague)
- Potential (research capability indicators)
