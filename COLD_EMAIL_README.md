## 💡 Lessons Learned

1. **Authenticity > Exaggeration:** Honest representation builds trust
2. **Specificity matters:** Generic emails are easy to ignore
3. **Clear connection:** Explain how your work relates to professor's research
4. **Research thoroughly:** Know the lab's current projects and application process
5. **Multiple reviews:** Devil's advocate approach catches issues early
6. **Professional tone:** Balance enthusiasm with respect
7. **Clear ask:** Be specific about what you want and offer
